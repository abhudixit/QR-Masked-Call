Frequently Asked Questions

How does QR Masked Call work?

Scan the QR code attached to a vehicle. The QR code identifies the vehicle and allows you to contact its owner through the available contact options.

Can I call the owner without seeing their number?

Yes. The final masked-call system will use a secure calling API so that the vehicle owner's personal phone number is not revealed.

Why does the website need camera permission?

Camera permission is required to scan the QR code directly from your device.

Does the website save my phone number?

The QR scanner itself does not require your phone number. Any future customer information will be handled separately through the secure backend.
