Frequently Asked Questions


How does QR Masked Call work?

Scan the QR code on the vehicle and choose the contact option. The owner's personal phone number is not displayed.


Can I see the owner's phone number?

No. The system is designed to keep the owner's phone number private.


Do I need to install an app?

No. The website can be opened directly from your phone or tablet.
